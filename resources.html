Social Security

Know your real benefit

Your benefit estimate is the single most important input in most retirement plans. Get it from the source.

  • my Social Security account

    ssa.gov

    Your personal benefit estimates at every claiming age, plus your full earnings record. Check it for missing years.

  • Full retirement age & early claiming

    ssa.gov

    How much your benefit shrinks if you claim before full retirement age, by birth year.

  • Quick benefit calculator

    ssa.gov

    A rough estimate without signing in. Useful for a spouse or for a quick what-if.

  • Cost-of-living adjustment (COLA)

    ssa.gov

    This year's Social Security raise, the earnings limit if you work while claiming, and the latest Medicare Part B premium.

Taxes

Current brackets and the rules behind them

Taxes are often the biggest expense in retirement that nobody budgets for.

  • Federal income tax rates & brackets

    irs.gov

    The current brackets for single and married filers, with a link to next year's inflation-adjusted figures when the IRS releases them.

  • Is my Social Security taxable? (Pub. 915)

    irs.gov

    Up to 85% of benefits can be taxed depending on your other income. This explains the thresholds and the worksheet.

  • Tax withholding estimator

    irs.gov

    Set the right withholding on a pension or a paycheck so you aren't surprised in April.

  • Investor.gov

    SEC

    Free, unbiased calculators and plain-language explainers from the Securities and Exchange Commission.

The plan's tax engine applies the brackets year by year and shows when a Roth conversion fills a low bracket instead of pushing you into a higher one.

IRAs, 401(k)s & RMDs

Limits and required withdrawals

  • Required minimum distributions (RMDs)

    irs.gov

    When you must start withdrawing from traditional IRAs and 401(k)s, how the amount is figured, and the penalty for missing one.

  • IRA contribution limits

    irs.gov

    This year's IRA limits, including the extra catch-up amount for age 50 and older, and the Roth income limits.

  • Planning for retirement

    CFPB

    Guides from the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau on pensions, reverse mortgages, choosing an adviser and when to claim.

  • Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation

    pbgc.gov

    If you have a private-sector pension: what's insured if the plan fails, and how to find a lost pension.

Medicare & health coverage

Before 65 and after

Healthcare is the cost most people underestimate, especially in the years before Medicare starts.

  • What Medicare costs this year

    medicare.gov

    Current premiums, deductibles and out-of-pocket limits for Parts A, B, D, Medicare Advantage and Medigap.

  • Medicare Plan Finder

    medicare.gov

    Compare drug plans and Medicare Advantage plans available in your ZIP code.

  • Lower your IRMAA surcharge

    ssa.gov

    Higher incomes pay more for Parts B and D. If retirement cut your income, you can ask Social Security to lower it with Form SSA-44.

  • Free Medicare counseling (SHIP)

    shiphelp.org

    Free, unbiased, one-on-one help from your state's health insurance assistance program. They don't sell anything.

  • HealthCare.gov

    healthcare.gov

    Marketplace coverage if you retire before 65. Your premium help depends on your income, which your withdrawal plan controls.

The planner's Medicare page estimates premiums, the IRMAA tiers and the pre-65 coverage gap, so they show up in your spending plan instead of surprising you.

Long-term care

Plan for care before you need it

  • Long-term care basics

    acl.gov

    What long-term care is, who pays for what, and what Medicare does and doesn't cover, from the Administration for Community Living.

  • Cost of Care Survey

    CareScout

    Home care, assisted living and nursing home costs where you live. This is the long-running survey many planners use.

  • Eldercare Locator

    acl.gov

    Find local services for older adults and caregivers, or call 1-800-677-1116.

Protect yourself

Check advisers and report scams

  • FINRA BrokerCheck

    finra.org

    Look up any broker or adviser's licenses, employment history and complaints before you hand over money.

  • Report fraud

    ftc.gov

    Report a scam to the Federal Trade Commission and get steps to protect yourself.
